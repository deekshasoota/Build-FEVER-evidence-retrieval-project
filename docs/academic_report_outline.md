# Academic report outline

1. Abstract: task, IR-focused method, dataset, measured results (only after experiments).
2. Introduction: misinformation verification and evidence retrieval motivation.
3. Literature review: FEVER task, lexical IR, BM25, textual entailment.
4. Problem formulation: claims, evidence sentences, verdict labels, evidence sets.
5. Dataset: official splits, Wikipedia snapshot, preprocessing, licensing and statistics.
6. Methodology: index, Boolean/TF-IDF/BM25, evidence aggregation, verifier.
7. Implementation: modular architecture, environment, reproducibility.
8. Experimental setup: split discipline, parameters, hardware and baselines.
9. Results: retrieval and verification metrics, only actual measurements.
10. Error analysis: retrieval misses, partial evidence, label confusion and NEI.
11. Limitations and ethics.
12. Conclusion and future work.
13. References, including the original FEVER paper.
